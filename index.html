LITTLE THINGS TO KNOW

Wedding Guest FAQ

♡ What time should I arrive?

We recommend arriving by 11:00 AM so you can settle in comfortably before our wedding celebration begins at 11:45 AM.

♡ Is the tea ceremony open to all guests?

Our tea ceremony will be an intimate moment shared with our closest family members. We look forward to celebrating with everyone at the wedding reception!

♡ What is the dress code?

Elegant / Smart Casual. We'd love for you to dress in something lovely yet comfortable for our special day. Come dressed to celebrate, smile and make beautiful memories with us!

♡ Can I bring a plus-one?

As we've carefully arranged seating for our guests, we kindly ask that you attend only with those named in your invitation. Thank you for understanding!

♡ Are children welcome?

Of course! Little ones included in your invitation are welcome to celebrate with us. A special kids' meal is available for children under 12. Kindly indicate this when completing your RSVP.

♡ Are vegetarian, halal or other dietary meals available?

Please let us know your dietary requirements when submitting your RSVP, and we'll do our best to make the necessary arrangements.

♡ When is the RSVP deadline?

Kindly confirm your attendance by Sunday, 3 January 2027, so we can finalise all the little details for our special day. ♡

♡ What if I can no longer attend after confirming my RSVP?

We understand that plans can sometimes change! If you're no longer able to join us, we'd really appreciate it if you could let Nicholas or Yen Ling know as soon as possible, so we can make the necessary adjustments to our arrangements. Thank you for keeping us updated!

♡ Can I share photos with the couple?

We'd love that! Scan the QR code on your wedding ticket to access our wedding-day guest portal and share your favourite moments with us. Every little memory means so much! ♡

♡ Who should I contact if I need help on the day?

Our friendly reception team will be happy to assist you at CHIJMES Hall. Feel free to approach them if you need any help.

With love, Nicholas & Yen Ling ♡
